Rights that help women thrive

Women’s rights are human rights. They support fairness, independence, participation, and the chance for every person to live with dignity.

⚖️

Equality and dignity

Women and girls deserve equal respect and protection from discrimination. Every person should be treated fairly and with dignity.

📚

Education and learning

Girls and women should have equal opportunity to learn, explore their interests, build skills, and pursue their goals.

🩺

Health and wellbeing

Women deserve access to respectful, informed healthcare and the ability to make decisions about their wellbeing.

💼

Fair work and opportunity

Women should have fair access to work, equal opportunity to grow, and workplaces free from harassment and discrimination.

🗣️

Voice and participation

Women have the right to express their views, take part in decisions, and contribute to public, community, and family life.

🛡️

Safety and freedom

Every woman has the right to live free from violence, abuse, coercion, and intimidation, at home and in public.

The foundations of equality

Rights belong to every woman and girl.

Rights are not privileges to be earned. They are part of human dignity. Laws and services can help protect them, while families and communities can help make equality part of everyday life.

You deserve support

If you need help, you are not alone.

If you or someone you know is facing violence, harassment, or immediate danger, reach out to a trusted person or service. You can ask for help even if you are unsure what to do next.

In India, use 112 for immediate emergency assistance. The Women Helpline 181 can connect women to support and information. If it is safer, ask a trusted person to call with you.

Fairness at work

Everyone deserves a respectful workplace.

People should be able to work, learn, and build careers without harassment or discrimination. A safe workplace takes clear policies, fair processes, respectful behaviour, and accessible ways to raise concerns.

In India, the Ministry of Women and Child Development’s SHe-Box portal provides a channel for workplace sexual harassment complaints. Learn about the process and seek qualified advice if you need help understanding your options.

Make respect part of work

Listen without blame. Take concerns seriously. Respect privacy. Follow appropriate reporting and support processes. Everyone has a role in creating a workplace where people can do their best work safely.

Wellbeing matters

Equality includes the freedom to care for yourself.

Good health includes physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing. Rest, respectful healthcare, supportive relationships, and freedom from fear all matter.

Asking for help is a sign of strength. If you are facing a health concern, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional. If you feel unsafe, reach out to someone you trust or a support service.
