If an emergency happens

Pause. Call. Share what matters.

You don’t need perfect words. Give the operator simple, useful facts and follow their instructions.

1
Move to a safer place, if you can.Do not put yourself at further risk. Ask someone nearby for help when it is safe.
2
Call 112 or the appropriate helpline.Say what happened and what kind of help is needed.
3
Give your location clearly.Share the area, street, nearby landmark, building name, and a call-back number.
4
Stay on the line and follow instructions.Answer questions as calmly as possible. Don’t hang up until the operator says it’s okay.
Everyday readiness

Stay healthy. Stay safe. Stay prepared.

A little preparation can make a difficult moment easier for you and the people around you.

📌
Save important contacts.Add 112 and trusted family or friends to your phone.
📍
Know where you are.Notice nearby landmarks and keep your address easy to find.
🔋
Keep your phone charged.A charged phone helps you contact help and share your location.
🧘

🤝
Check in with others.Make a simple plan with family, neighbours, or caregivers.
✅
Use helplines responsibly.Keep emergency lines clear for people who urgently need help.
A quick note

Numbers can vary by location.

India’s 112 service is the nationwide single emergency number for urgent police, fire, health and other emergency response. Some traditional or specialised numbers may also work, but coverage and handling can differ by State/UT. For life-threatening situations, use 112 first.
