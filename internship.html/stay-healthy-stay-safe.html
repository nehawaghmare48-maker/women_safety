The everyday essentials

Healthy habits, happier you

You don’t need a perfect routine to feel better. Start with simple habits that fit real life, and let small wins add up.

🥗

Eat a rainbow

Choose a variety of colourful fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and nourishing foods. Enjoy meals slowly and listen to your body.

💧

Drink enough water

Keep water nearby and sip throughout the day. Staying hydrated supports your body as you move, work, and rest.

🚶

Move your way

Walk, stretch, dance, cycle, or play. Regular movement can boost energy and mood—find something you genuinely enjoy.

🌙

Make room for sleep

A calming bedtime routine and a regular sleep schedule can help you wake up feeling more rested and ready for the day.

🧼

Keep clean, feel good

Wash your hands with soap, care for your teeth, and keep the spaces you use clean. Little acts of hygiene matter.

🩺

Stay on top of care

Pay attention to how you feel and keep up with recommended check-ups. Ask a qualified health professional when you have concerns.

Everyday awareness

Safety is an act of care.

Look after yourself and the people around you with thoughtful, everyday choices.

🪖
Use protective gear.
Wear a seat belt in the car and a helmet when riding.
🚦
Stay alert on the road.
Follow traffic rules and put your phone away while driving.
☀️
Be sun smart.
Seek shade, wear protective clothing, and use sunscreen as directed.
📱
Protect your digital space.
Use strong passwords and think before sharing personal information.
🧯
Know your surroundings.
Learn local emergency contacts and follow trusted safety guidance.
🤝
Look out for one another.
Check in, offer help, and speak up when something feels unsafe.
A healthy mind matters

Give your mind the same care you give your body.

Rest, connection, and moments of calm are part of wellbeing too. Make space for what restores you, and remember that asking for support is a strong step.

Everyone’s experience is different. If stress or low mood is affecting daily life, consider talking with someone you trust or a qualified professional.
